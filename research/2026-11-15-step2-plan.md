# Step 2 plan: simulation, test and gap closure on v1

2026-11-15

## Where performance stands today (before Step 2)

The latest like-for-like fleet comparison ran on 2026-11-14, on the long-pass routes with held-out draws. It used the **old compact-XPS wing (2.29 kg)**, not v1. On that run, Lodestar SiCore is:

- **tied with the DeltaQuad Evo GOV at 3 hours on the 3 km route** (+0.34 points to the Evo, interval spans zero);
- **behind the Evo GOV by 1.17 points at 3 hours on the 6 km route** (interval excludes zero);
- **ahead at 1 hour on the 3 km route** by 0.45 points, but not resolved;
- **clearly ahead of the Quantum Trinity Pro and the eBee X** at 3 hours.

**The honest summary:** today Lodestar is competitive, not leading. Its gain over a well-funded fielded drone comes from the kit drop and the price, not from search reach. Step 2 measures v1 and finds out where it genuinely leads.

## Rules that make the numbers credible

1. **Fix the metrics and the scenario set before running.** Every Step 2 headline metric, scenario and comparison is listed in this note. Nothing is added after seeing results without being labelled post hoc.
2. **Held-out draws only for headline numbers.** Tuning uses seed 20261111. Headline runs use seed 20261114, plus one new seed, 20261115, never used for tuning.
3. **Give every competitor its best case.** Use published endurance, its best search speed and the same route planner. Show the 80%-endurance sensitivity alongside, not instead.
4. **Report distributions, not points.** Endurance and mission metrics get P10/P50/P90 bands from a parameter Monte Carlo covering:
   - drag;
   - propulsive efficiency;
   - cell capacity and resistance;
   - mass growth.

## Gap audit: what an engineering team would expect that the repo does not yet have

The checklists are the preliminary design review (PDR) entry criteria from the NASA SE Handbook (NASA/SP-2016-6105, App. G), MIL-STD-882E system safety and SAE ARP4761 safety assessment, scaled to a small UAS. The status column records the check made today.

| # | Item | Status today | Step 2 action |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Functional hazard assessment with a risk matrix (882E severity × probability) | Missing | Write the FHA covering:<ul><li>loss of thrust;</li><li>loss of control;</li><li>loss of link;</li><li>loss of navigation;</li><li>kit misrelease;</li><li>battery thermal runaway;</li><li>structural failure;</li><li>mid-air with a manned aircraft.</li></ul> |
| G2 | FMEA, component level | Missing | Cover the motor, ESC, packs, servos, linkage, joint, release, parachute, autopilot, GPS, pitot, radio and power distribution. Each row gets its effect, how it is detected, the mitigation and the residual risk. |
| G3 | Contingency behaviours, physical side: glide range after motor-out, link-loss return, flight termination | Partial (bingo rule only) | Glide polar, reachable-area maps on the 7 sites, a termination descent-energy estimate |
| G4 | Command and control link budget in mountain terrain | Missing; the terrain sim only has camera line of sight | Run a free-space-plus-terrain-masking link budget, with a ground relay option, on the 7 sites. Report the fraction of each route with link. |

| G6 | v1 6-DOF model: trim across the envelope, Dryden gusts (MIL-F-8785C), kit release, motor-out | The JSBSim model is still v2/v3 | Rebuild the JSBSim aircraft from `lodestar_v1.py` and rerun release, turbulence and motor-out |

Items deliberately out of scope, per Dominic's notes:
- physical hardware and tests (the 8 tests listed in the compliance note stay as the test plan);
- autopilot software;

## Order of work

1. **Close G14, then bring the mission sim onto v1.** The sim reads mass, power and stall from `lodestar_v1.py`, and `freeze_check` gets a mission section.
2. **Headline runs.** P1, P2 and P5 on held-out seeds, full fleet, both radii, both route modes; P3 and P4 with the Monte Carlo; P6 and P7 from the performance model.
3. **G6:** rebuild and rerun the v1 6-DOF model.
4. **G1–G4, G9:** safety and contingency analyses. The 6-DOF and terrain tools feed G3, G4 and G9.
5. **G5, G7, G8, G10, G12, G13:** consolidation documents.
6. **Step 2 report:** results against the pre-registered metrics, where Lodestar leads and trails and why, and the candidate list for Step 3.

**Done when:**
- every metric in the table above has a number with an interval;
- every gap row is closed or explicitly deferred with a reason;
- the full test suite still passes;
- `freeze_check` covers the mission results.
