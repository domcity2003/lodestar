# Lodestar v1 (revision v1.1): requirements compliance

2026-11-16

This note updates the [v1 compliance note](2026-11-14-v1-requirements-compliance.md) and the [verification matrix](2026-11-15-verification-matrix.md) for revision v1.1, the released v1 design. v1.1 is the v1.0 freeze plus the Step 2 changes in [the Step 2 results](2026-11-16-step2-results.md) §3. Those notes still hold for everything not listed here: the derived requirements E1–E21, fixes F1–F15, limits L1–L7, the certification basis and tests T1–T8.

## Answer

Revision v1.1 meets **11 of 13** top-level requirements by analysis.
- **Deviations:** the same three as v1 (D1–D3).
- **R13 (parts ≤ $8k) is deferred** by decision on 2026-11-16: best performance first, with a cost-down pass later. Parts are $16.9k including airframe materials.
- **Thinnest compliant margins:** R1 at a fixed 20 m/s search speed (2.97 h, met at best speed with 3.49 h) and R10 with the mass growth allowance (9.82 kg against 10 kg).

Every value below is read from an output that `freeze_check.py` ties to `scripts/lodestar_v1.py` (VERSION v1.1).

## 3. Verification status

- `run_all_tests.py --full`: 24 scripts pass.
- `freeze_check.py` passes. It includes a mission section that checks each headline run's inputs against the frozen design.
- The verification matrix rows R1–R13 take the values in Table 1. E22–E27 and L8–L12 are verified by analysis, with the evidence listed in Table 2.
- No v1.0 row changes status.

Tests still needed before the claims become measured values:
- T1–T8 from the v1 note;
- weigh the aircraft parachute and get its packed volume;
- weigh the A12-S HV servos;
- measure the SiCore cell resistance (T5), which the high-DA climb margin depends on.
