# Wing finite-element verification and margins of safety

2026-11-15

## Answer

The v1 wing passes every flight, landing and ground case with a positive margin of safety. The FE model forced three changes to get there.

1. **The skin-wrinkling check was unconservative.** The beam sizing compared only spanwise compression with the skin's wrinkling stress. The FE shows that torsion shear of 9–12 MPa, combined with that compression, gives a minimum principal stress of 19.4 MPa on the inboard upper skin, against a 14.4 MPa allowable (margin −0.26). The fix:
   - the caps are now sized on principal stress, with a 1.10 FE correlation factor inboard of y = 0.30 m;
   - one extra ±45 glass ply goes on the centre-section upper skin (y = 0–0.45 m), dropped off over the next 50 mm.

   Lowest flight margin now: **+0.12**.
2. **A hard belly landing broke the root.** With 10 mm of crush stroke (pod on hard ground), the root lower skin was at −0.64 and the lower cap at −0.28. An **EPP belly skid with at least 40 mm stroke** brings every landing margin to +0.7 or better. At 30 mm, the root skin is still 3% short.
3. **Six construction details are mandatory.** The FE runs each one both ways. Without the detail the margin is negative; with it the margin is positive (Table 3). These are recorded in `lodestar_v1.STRUCTURE["construction_details"]`.

The wing grows from 1.881 to **1.983 kg** (15.4 g/dm²). The aircraft is 8.31 kg loaded (SiCore), and the static margin is 7.1% loaded and 7.4% released.

## Model

`scripts/fea_wing.py` writes a CalculiX 2.22 deck from Python and post-processes the results; `scripts/fea_extra.py` runs the load-case matrix and the margins table.

**The model** is one half-wing, from the centreline to the winglet root, swept and tapered, with MH45 sections:
- S4 shells for the ±45 glass skins (orthotropic, G12 = 9 GPa);
- the carbon caps smeared into the skin strip over the spar;
- the Rohacell spar web at 25% chord and the XPS rear web at 72% chord;
- C3D8I and C3D6 solids for the foam core: EPS 20, with XPS at the servo bay and the winglet root.

**Boundary conditions and masses.** The wing is clamped at the centreline, as the beam model is. The model carries the beam model's masses: the packs at the frozen layout, the joints, the servo, the winglet and the elevon balance.

**Loads** are the ultimate cases from `out/wing_trade/v1_loads.csv` (limit × 1.5). The load reconstruction reproduces the beam's shear, bending and torque within 2% at every station.

**Table 1.** Mesh convergence by quantity, governing positive-gust case. Run on the first-frozen wing, before the doubler.

| Quantity | Coarse (1,460 nodes) | Medium (8,970 nodes, used) | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Max upper-skin min principal stress [MPa] | 19.63 | 19.40 | 1.2% |
| Ultimate tip deflection [mm] | 44.1 | 43.8 | 0.7% |
| First bending [Hz] | 12.29 | 12.33 | 0.3% |
| First torsion [Hz] | 46.52 | 46.62 | 0.2% |

The fine mesh (about 36,000 nodes) did not finish within the 1-hour solver limit. The medium mesh is used for every result.

**Table 2.** Clamped natural frequency by mode and model [Hz], final v1 wing with SiCore packs.

| Mode | FE shell/solid | Beam, projected span | Beam, swept length |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bending 1 | 13.3 | 16.0 | 13.8 |
| Torsion 1 | 49.4 | 46.8 | 41.5 |
| Bending 2 | 38.9 | n/a | n/a |

The beam model's first bending frequency agrees with the FE once it is laid along the swept spar: 13.8 vs 13.3 Hz. The projected-span beam overstates it by 20%, because it shortens the wing by cos 25°. The flutter work uses the FE frequencies.

**Known-answer tests** (`scripts/test_fea_wing.py`, 5 checks):
- a shell box against Euler-Bernoulli bending and Bredt torsion, within 3%, including the orthotropic glass orientation;
- the box's first frequency;
- the load reconstruction;
- the wing FE mass against the beam section table.

## Margins of safety

Source: `out/fea/margins.csv`, 45 rows. MS = allowable / applied − 1, at ultimate load. Every allowable is an assumed, knocked-down material value, and each row says which. The coupon tests in the compliance note replace them.

**Table 3.** Lowest margin by element group, final v1 wing.

| Element | Failure mode | Governing case | MS |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Upper skin on EPS | Face wrinkling, min principal | Gust + at Vc, y = 0.71 m | +0.12 |
| Joint tube 20 × 16 | Ovalization at the sleeve mouth (needs hoop plies) | Gust + at Vc | +0.19 |
| Wing-pod saddle | Bearing (G10 plates specified; this row is the bare-EPS case) | Belly landing, 40 mm skid | +0.01 |
| Lower skin on EPS | Face wrinkling, flight | Gust − at VD | +0.50 |
| Spar cap, upper | Wrinkling on the Rohacell web | Gust + at Vc | +0.68 |
| Joint tube 20 × 16 | Bending, fixed-aileron sensitivity | 4,400 m, no roll relief | +0.81 |
| Battery-bay walls | Pack inertia bearing on EPS | Bungee launch 10 g | +0.65 |
| Lower skin on EPS | Wrinkling, belly landing | 40 mm skid, root | +0.72 |
| Tip deflection at limit load | 5% of semispan | Envelope | +1.9 |

## Limits of this analysis

- **Static, linear, ultimate load.** There is no progressive failure or post-buckling. Face wrinkling uses the Hoff formula with a 0.3 coefficient, which is the main assumption to test (coupon T1).
- **The clamp at the centreline** stiffens the root slightly, as in the beam model. The pod saddle and its G10 plates are checked by hand, not in the mesh.
- **The doubler is modelled as a thickness step**, with a 1.5× drop-off band. A real ply drop is tapered; the step is the conservative case.
- **The elevon is modelled as continuous with the wing,** held by the servo. Elevon flutter is in the [flutter note](2026-11-14-flutter-verification.md).

## Reproduce

From `scripts/`:
- `../.venv/Scripts/python.exe -X utf8 fea_wing.py`: modes, statics, load-case matrix and margins, about 10–20 minutes.
- `../.venv/Scripts/python.exe -X utf8 test_fea_wing.py`: known-answer checks.

Outputs go to `out/fea/`: `margins.csv`, `modes.csv`, `fe_vs_beam.csv`, `envelope_fe.csv`, `landing_cases.csv`, `joint_checks.csv` and `run.log`.
