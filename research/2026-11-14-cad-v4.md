# CAD v4 rebuild in FreeCAD, with corrected mass properties

2026-11-14

## Answer

- **The CAD is back online.** FreeCAD 1.1.4 is driven headless from the project venv through a small reusable helper, `scripts/fcad/`, chosen after a survey of scripted CAD tools (CadQuery and build123d were blocked by their OCP dependency on this machine).
- **The v4 model is built at the v1 layout,** with the wing packs 25 mm forward and 25 mm inboard and the compact 20 mm joint with sleeves.
  - Every internal part fits inside its skin with the 1.5 mm skin allowance.
  - Nothing interferes.
  - The prop disc clears the wing by 40 mm.
- **The port uncovered a kernel bug.** OpenCASCADE computes the volume of spline airfoil lofts 12–20% low. The archived v3 CAD balance check carried this error: its wing centroid was 45 mm too far aft.
  - The v1 balance does not depend on that error. `wing_trade.py` models the wing's mass along the span rather than from the CAD solid.
  - With the CAD v4 component positions, v1 balances at **x = 0.4146 m** (SiCore, loaded) and **0.4127 m** after the kit drops.
  - That gives a **6.8% / 7.1%** static margin, which restores the 6% target.

## Update, later on 2026-11-14: re-trim for the elevon balance

The flutter analysis added a 60 g mass balance per elevon, and the propulsion work added a 0.10 kg pack thermal kit. Both now appear as rows in `mass_cg.csv`. The balance weights sit at x = 0.715 m, so they moved the CG 4.5 mm aft. To win that back, the wing packs moved a further 15 mm forward and 25 mm inboard, to x = 0.412 m and y = 0.305 m.

At the new layout:
- the fit check passes;
- cell to inboard sleeve is 5.3 mm (screening estimate 4.3 mm), and cell to skin after the allowance is 2.7 mm;
- SiCore weighs 8.215 kg loaded and 6.591 kg released, with the CG at 0.4144 and 0.4126 m;
- the static margin is 6.83% and 7.16% (`out/wing_trade/aircraft_mass_balance_v1.csv`).

Tables 2 and 3 above show the earlier layout.

## Gotchas worth keeping

These are in `scripts/fcad/README.md`. They apply to any CAD project on this machine.

- **Never trust `Shape.Volume` on spline geometry.** Integrate over a fine mesh instead (about 0.1% accuracy).
- **Use loft max degree 8** to match build123d. FreeCAD's default of 5 bulges the pod by 4%.
- **Open the trailing edge.** A trailing-edge closing line near 0.1 mm makes an invalid solid.
- **FreeCADCmd quirks:**
  - extra arguments are treated as files to open, so pass the job path through `FCAD_JOB`;
  - uncaught exceptions still exit 0, so `fc_build.py` traps them.

## Reproduce

From the repo root:
- `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -X utf8 scripts/cad_v4.py` builds v4, in about 4 minutes.
- `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -X utf8 scripts/cad_v4.py --v3` runs the reproduction, in about 14 minutes.
- `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -X utf8 scripts/test_fcad.py` runs the known-answer checks.

Outputs go to `out/cad_v4/`: `lodestar_v4.step`, `stl/`, `fit_check.csv`, `joint_gaps.csv`, `mass_cg.csv`, `part_props.csv`, `v3_reproduction.csv` and `renders/`.
